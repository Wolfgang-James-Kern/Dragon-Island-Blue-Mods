# Easy Underworld Entrance

This is a manifest-format mod for a Dragon Island Blue Mod Launcher. It adds a new portal POI at `(313, 262)`, slightly north of the Unknown Relic, and a matching Underworld portal at `(210, 62)`. Both are connected to their local world graphs and provide direct travel in both directions.

The new portal intentionally bypasses the stock `CaiusDefeated` condition and Dungeon 16 traversal when entering the Underworld.
